Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

NARA Studio is a private tool used only by its owner. It does not serve other users and does not collect data from TikTok viewers.

Data used

  • The owner's TikTok access and refresh tokens and account ID, used only to send videos to the owner's account.
  • Video files and captions made by the owner.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold or shared with anyone. Videos are sent only to TikTok.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time in TikTok settings under Manage app permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted.
