Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

NARA Studio is a private tool used only by its owner. It does not serve other users and does not collect data from TikTok or YouTube viewers.

Data used

  • The owner's TikTok access and refresh tokens and account ID, used only to send videos to the owner's account.
  • The owner's Google access and refresh tokens, used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to read those channels' names and IDs, and to read and update the owner's own production spreadsheets.
  • Video files, titles, descriptions and captions made by the owner.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored on the owner's own computer. Data is not sold or shared with anyone. Videos are sent only to TikTok and to the owner's YouTube channels.

Google user data

NARA Studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only for the features above, is not used for advertising, is not transferred to anyone, and is not used to train AI models.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time: in TikTok settings under Manage app permissions, and for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted.
